ABOUT US

TEACHERS + AI

       Interdisciplinary Battles Teachers + AI is an educational initiative developed in collaboration with the ULYTAU Foundation, Canadian International School Astana, and the Akimat of Astana. This project aims to empower teachers from rural schools across Kazakhstan by introducing them to the practical applications of Artificial Intelligence in education. As part of the initiative, we conducted workshops and created YouTube video tutorials that reached over 85 schools nationwide.

       Through these interactive sessions, we demonstrate how educators can effectively integrate platforms like Gamma AI, Gemini AI, Perplexity, and other AI tools into their daily teaching practice. Our goal is to help teachers streamline lesson planning, simplify content creation, and reduce time spent on routine tasks, so they can focus on what matters most: building strong, meaningful relationships with their students.

       We also address a growing challenge in today’s classrooms: AI-assisted cheating. By helping teachers understand how students may misuse AI tools, we equip them to develop smarter, more adaptive assessments that uphold academic integrity. At the core of this initiative is our commitment to educational equity: we believe the quality of education should not be determined by geography. Whether in cities or rural communities, every teacher deserves access to the same tools and opportunities to help their students thrive.

SUMMER 2025 KOFI ANNAN AWARD

In honor of Kofi Annan — former Secretary-General of the United Nations, Nobel Peace Prize laureate, and advocate for peace, human dignity, and youth leadership — we launch this global essay challenge to confront the questions others avoid.

Kofi Annan believed in the power of young people to shape the world and in the importance of asking hard questions.

Essay Topic

“What is a question that is scary to ask — but needs to be asked? Maybe it feels taboo. It may seem too obvious to matter. Perhaps it’s so neglected that we stopped noticing. We’re calling on young voices to revive these hidden questions — and tell us why the world needs to hear them now.”

  • 2nd Place: Olivia Davis (United States of America)

May 2024 STEAM Battles Lowell High School Canada

This time, STEAM Battles focused on sustainable architecture. Students had a limited time to explore and create a prototype of a sustainable home, using ecological materials, renewable energy, and other sustainable practices.

Relevance to Subjects

  • Physics: Insulation, energy efficiency
  • Chemistry: Useful chemical reactions, green chemistry, dynamic equilibrium
  • Biology: Safeguarding biodiversity, ecosystems, and the impact of architectural design on human health and well-being
  • Design: Eco-home design
  • Technology: Sensors and technological tools for sustainable homes
  • Arts: Impact of colors, shapes, and artistic elements on well-being; exploring architectural styles of different eras and their ecological impact (e.g., Gaudí’s work)
  • CAS: Creativity
  • FPPL / Local Communities: Investigating indigenous techniques and community-driven sustainable architecture
December 2023 STEAM Battles 1.0

The initiative of STEAM Battles aims to revolutionize the education landscape by equipping high school and middle school students with practical skills, teamwork abilities, and communication competencies essential for their future success. The implementation strategy, which is already underway, involves coordinating efforts amongst various academic institutions globally to transform STEAM Battles into an international endeavor.

The second stage of the inaugural event brought together 95 individuals from all over Kazakhstan (Astana, Almaty, Karaganda, and Atyrau) and received over 200 applications to attend the event on the first stage. The preparatory phase took 3 months and included over 30 volunteers. We acquired over 100 kg of props. The objective of the hands-on project was that each team had to build a construction capable of holding 30 kg (66 lbs). Additionally, by including a financial literacy component—using our inter-olympiad currency—participants improved their budgeting and money-management skills.

November 2023 Charity Fair

In just one day, our Charity Fair raised $400 to support our organisation’s mission. By providing students with a platform to showcase and sell their goods—ranging from pastries to handcrafted tools—we engaged over 70 individuals in our fundraising efforts. The Ulytau Foundation, after witnessing our work, generously contributed an additional $600.

September – October 2023 Eco Challenge

In the span of two months, we collaborated with all classes to hold an Eco Challenge. This initiative promoted sustainability and student agency by incorporating responsible consumption and production, sustainable communities, and climate action—key pillars of the SDGs. Students collected paper, cardboard, and plastic bottles as props for a culminating event, resulting in approximately 100 kg of paper/cardboard and 50 kg of plastic gathered.
